B · Post-Trauma Radiography Rules, Distal Biceps Avulsion & Red-Flag TriageTRIAGE GATE
Elbow Extension Test & Trauma ROM RulesAppelboam 2008
Elbow extension test (Appelboam 2008 BMJ, adults + children, n = 1740) H: fracture: Sn 96.8% (95.0–98.2), Sp 48.5% (45.6–51.4); worst-case Sn 95.3%; Sp similar in adults (47.7%) and children (49.5%)
Elbow extension test — adults (Appelboam 2008 BMJ) H: full extension: NPV 98.4% (96.3–99.5), LR− 0.03 (0.01–0.08); radiography can be deferred if an olecranon fracture is not suspected; patients not X-rayed “should return if symptoms have not resolved within 7-10 days”
Range of movement and the radiograph (Lennon 2007 Emerg Med J) M: for detecting a normal radiograph: full extension Sp 0.916 (0.863–0.969), Sn 0.478; equal ROM Sp 0.976 (0.940–0.991), Sn 0.211; under 16 years equal ROM Sp 1 (0.941–1.000)
Active ROM after trauma (Darracq 2008 Am J Emerg Med, n = 113) M: limited active ROM (flexion, extension, pronation, supination): Sn 100% (0.93–1.00) for fracture or effusion; preserved active ROM: Sp 97% (0.89–1.00) for absence of fracture
Distal Biceps Avulsion & Emergency Red FlagsO'Driscoll 2007
Hook test — distal biceps avulsion (O'Driscoll 2007 Am J Sports Med, n = 45, surgery reference) M: Sn and Sp both 100%; MRI Sn 92%, Sp 85% · Biceps squeeze test (Ruland 2005 Clin Orthop Relat Res) L: positive in 24 of 25 patients; complete rupture confirmed at surgery in 21 of 22
Dislocation & compartment syndrome: dislocation with neurovascular compromise (absent pulse, pallor, acute nerve deficit); forearm compartment syndrome (severe pain out of proportion, tense swollen forearm, pain on passive finger stretch) E
Septic & paediatric red flags: septic olecranon bursitis / septic arthritis (fever, heat, swelling); fracture in a child (acute trauma, refusal to use arm, swelling or incomplete active ROM) E
Visceral & proximal referral: cardiac referral to the left medial arm (non-mechanical ache, chest/jaw symptoms) E · cervical radiculopathy referral (C5–T1 neck/radicular screen) E
Medication & Modality Safety: Houghton 2010 (Guideline): electrophysical contraindications (malignancy, active DVT, pregnancy, electronic implants) • Kearns 2019 E: anticoagulants / coagulopathy — caution — possible increased incidence or severity of haematoma and bruising • Wise 2012 M: fluoroquinolones: tendonitis OR 4.3, rupture OR 2.0; stronger with oral glucocorticoids (OR 9.1) and age > 60 (OR 8.3) • Waters 2023 L: quinolones after distal biceps repair: prescription within 90 days: reoperation 3.6 % vs 1.7 % (OR 2.13)
TRIAGE DISPOSITION: Cleared — proceed Radiography required (incomplete extension / trauma / child) Urgent surgical referral (distal biceps avulsion) Emergency referral (dislocation / compartment syndrome / septic / cardiac)
