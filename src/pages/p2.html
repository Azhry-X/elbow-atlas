B · Prevalence, Risk Factors & Natural Course vs Corticosteroid Injection / PhysiotherapyPROGNOSIS
Epidemiology & Risk Factors (Shiri 2006)Shiri 2006
Prevalence (age 30–64, n = 4,783) (Shiri 2006 Am J Epidemiol) H: definite lateral epicondylitis 1.3%, medial 0.4%; highest at 45–54 years; no sex difference
Lateral epicondylitis — risk factors (Shiri 2006 Am J Epidemiol) H: current smoking OR 3.4 (1.4–8.3); former smoking OR 3.0 (1.3–6.6); repetitive + forceful arm activity OR 5.6 (1.9–16.5)
Natural Course vs Injection & Physiotherapy RCTsBisset 2006
Natural course vs treatment (RCT, n = 185) (Smidt 2002 Lancet) H: success at 6 weeks: injection 92%, physiotherapy 47%, wait-and-see 32%; at 52 weeks: injection 69%, physiotherapy 91%, wait-and-see 83%
MWM + exercise vs injection vs wait-and-see (RCT, n = 198) (Bisset 2006 BMJ) H: at 52 weeks injection worse than physiotherapy (0.3, 0.1 to 0.5; NNT 4) and than wait-and-see on 2 of 3 measures (0.3, 0.04 to 0.4; NNT 4); after injection 47/65 (72%) deteriorated after 3 or 6 weeks
C · Diagnostic Accuracy Summary: Cubital Tunnel, Ulnar Collateral Ligament & PLRIACCURACY
Cubital Tunnel Provocative Tests (Novak 1994 · Cheng 2008)Ulnar Nerve
Cubital tunnel provocative tests (Novak 1994 J Hand Surg Am, 32 patients, EMG-proven) M: Tinel Sn 0.70; at 30 seconds: elbow flexion 0.32, pressure 0.55, pressure + flexion 0.91 (most sensitive)
Scratch collapse test (Cheng 2008 J Hand Surg Am) L: cubital tunnel: Sn 69% (Tinel 54%, flexion/compression 46%), accuracy 89%; Tinel NPV 98%; carpal tunnel Sn 64%
PLRI Apprehension Signs (Regan 2006)LUCL / PLRI
PLRI apprehension signs (n = 8) (Regan 2006 J Shoulder Elbow Surg) L: chair sign positive in 7, push-up sign positive in 7; pivot-shift positive awake in 3 (all under anaesthesia); chair + push-up more sensitive than awake pivot-shift
UCL Moving Valgus & Milking Tests (O'Driscoll 2005 · Sciascia 2026)Medial / UCL
UCL — moving valgus stress test (O'Driscoll 2005 Am J Sports Med, n = 21, surgery / arthroscopy reference) L: Sn 100% (17/17), Sp 75% (3/4); pain arc 120° to 70°, maximum at 90°
UCL — moving valgus vs milking (Sciascia 2026 Am J Sports Med) M: moving valgus stress test Sn 98%, accuracy 93% (best to rule out); milking manoeuvre LR+ 5.1, Sn 77% (best to rule in); combining tests added nothing
