B · JOSPT LET CPG Manual Therapy & Dry Needling Quotes + Cubital Tunnel Conservative CareCPG & NEURAL
JOSPT LET CPG — Manual Therapy & Dry Needling (Lucado 2022)JOSPT 2022
Local elbow manipulation / mobilisation (Lucado 2022 JOSPT LET CPG) (Guideline): “Clinicians should use local elbow joint manipulation or mobilization techniques to reduce pain and increase pain-free grip strength … for those who can tolerate the specific technique”
Cervical, thoracic & wrist adjuncts (Lucado 2022 JOSPT LET CPG) (Guideline): “may use manipulation or mobilization techniques directed at the cervical spine, thoracic spine, and/or wrist as an adjunct to local treatment for short-term pain relief … when impairments in those regions are identified”
IASTM & deep transverse friction (Lucado 2022 JOSPT LET CPG) (Guideline): “may use instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilization combined with exercise to improve pain and function in those with chronic LET”; deep transverse friction: “a recommendation cannot be made”
Dry needling (Lucado 2022 JOSPT LET CPG) (Guideline): “Clinicians should use either tendon or trigger point dry needling for the treatment of pain and functional deficits associated with LET”
Cubital Tunnel Syndrome Conservative CareE
Patient education & posture modification E: avoid sustained elbow flexion, leaning on the medial epicondyle / retrocondylar groove, and repetitive flexion-extension under load
Night splinting / extension towel wrap E: maintain comfortable elbow extension during sleep to reduce nocturnal ulnar nerve traction and intraneural pressure
Neural mobilisation E: gentle, non-provocative ulnar nerve gliding exercises; monitor intrinsic hand strength and refer if progressive motor deficit develops
C · Dry Needling Safety & Adverse Events SAFETY SCREEN
Adverse event rates: Brady 2014 M: mild AEs 19.18 %; upper risk rate for significant AEs ≤ 0.04 %; bruising 7.55 %, bleeding 4.65 % • Boyce 2020 M: minor AE 36.7 %; major AE < .1 % (prolonged symptom aggravation .03 %, fainting .02 %, forgotten needles .01 %)
Serious risks & screening: Halle 2016 E: pneumothorax, cardiac tamponade, haematoma, infection, CNS injury; filament needles 32–36 gauge (.25–.20 mm) • Kearns 2019 E: anticoagulants / coagulopathy — caution — possible increased incidence or severity of haematoma and bruising
